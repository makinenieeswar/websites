About Eeswar

Eeswar Makineni is the founder and CEO of MET-Ai Solutions — a startup at the cutting edge of healthcare artificial intelligence. His journey began with a single, powerful question: Why does one of the world's most critical industries still operate on manual, error-prone processes?

That question drove him to build MET-Ai Solutions — a company that combines Medi Electronic Technology with the full power of Artificial Intelligence to automate, predict, and transform every facet of hospital care.

Eeswar's vision goes beyond software. He is building the intelligent infrastructure — hardware, software, and AI — that future hospitals will run on. A system where every patient is monitored continuously, every diagnosis is AI-assisted, and every decision is backed by data.

The MET-Ai Vision

Eeswar founded MET-Ai with a phased roadmap to total hospital transformation:

  • Phase 1 — AI-powered patient intake and triage automation
  • Phase 2 — Intelligent diagnostics across all modalities
  • Phase 3 — Automated, personalized treatment planning
  • Phase 4 — 24/7 predictive monitoring and early warning
  • Phase 5 — The fully autonomous, AI-run hospital

Each phase builds on the last — creating a compounding intelligence platform that grows smarter with every patient interaction.

Why MET-Ai?

The name says it all: Medi for medicine, Electronic for the hardware layer, Technology for the systems — and AI for the intelligence that binds it all together.

Eeswar believes that the future of healthcare is not just digital — it is intelligent. Sensors that never sleep. Algorithms that never tire. Systems that learn from every patient to serve the next one better.

MET-Ai is not just a software company. It is a full-stack healthcare intelligence company — from the sensor on the patient's wrist to the AI model generating the diagnosis.
