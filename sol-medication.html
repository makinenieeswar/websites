Eliminating Medication Errors with AI

Medication errors are one of the leading causes of preventable patient harm globally. MET-Ai intercepts errors at every stage — prescribing, dispensing, administering, and monitoring — before they can reach the patient.

Our system cross-references every prescription against the patient's allergy profile, current medications, renal and hepatic function, genomic metabolism data, and thousands of known drug interactions — in milliseconds.

  • Real-time allergy and contraindication checking at prescribing
  • Multi-drug interaction analysis across the full medication list
  • Weight and renal function-adjusted dose recommendations
  • Pharmacogenomic compatibility checking (CYP450 enzyme profiling)

Automated Dispensing and Administration

Beyond prescribing, MET-Ai automates the physical dispensing and administration process. Our integration with automated dispensing cabinets and smart IV pumps ensures the right drug, right dose, right route, and right time — every time.

Barcode scanning and RFID verification at the bedside create a closed-loop medication system where every administration event is electronically verified.

  • Automated dispensing cabinet integration with AI picking verification
  • Smart IV pump programming with auto-rate-check against orders
  • Bedside barcode verification: right patient, right drug, right time
  • Controlled substance tracking with chain-of-custody audit trail
