Continuous Watch

Always Watching, Always Learning

MET-Ai's Remote Patient Monitoring platform connects every patient to an always-on surveillance network. Whether a patient is in the ICU, on a general ward, recovering at home, or in a rural clinic — their vitals are continuously monitored and AI-analyzed in real time.

Our platform ingests data from wearable sensors, bedside monitors, implantable devices, and home health equipment — fusing all signals into a unified, real-time patient view.

  • Continuous ECG, SpO2, blood pressure, temperature, and respiratory rate
  • Wireless transmission from bedside devices to central AI dashboard
  • Wearable patch sensors for general ward and post-discharge monitoring
  • Implantable device integration: pacemakers, CGMs, cardiac monitors

Hospital-at-Home and Post-Discharge Care

The hospital is no longer four walls. MET-Ai's remote monitoring extends clinical-grade care into patients' homes — enabling safe early discharge, virtual ward rounds, and proactive readmission prevention.

Our platform supports the full hospital-at-home model: remote monitoring, AI triage, virtual consultations, and medication reminders — all managed from the hospital's central dashboard.

  • Post-surgical home recovery monitoring with daily AI health reports
  • Chronic disease management: diabetes, COPD, heart failure, hypertension
  • Paediatric home monitoring with parent-facing mobile notifications
  • Virtual ward round support — review patient data remotely with full context
