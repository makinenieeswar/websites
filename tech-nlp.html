Clinical NLP Engine

Natural Language Processing allows computers to read and understand human language. In healthcare, most clinical knowledge is locked in unstructured text — doctor's notes, discharge summaries, and patient histories.

MET-Ai's NLP engine transforms this text into coded, searchable medical data — surfacing diagnoses, medications, allergies, and clinical events automatically.

  • Automatic ICD-10 and SNOMED code extraction
  • Medication name, dose, and frequency recognition
  • Clinical entity extraction from free-text notes
  • Sentiment and urgency analysis in patient communications

Voice AI for Clinical Documentation

Physicians spend up to 2 hours daily on documentation for every hour of patient care. MET-Ai's Voice AI lets clinicians dictate naturally during consultations, generating a complete structured clinical note automatically.

Our voice models are trained on medical terminology and multilingual accents — ensuring accuracy even in fast-paced clinical settings.

  • Ambient clinical intelligence — passive recording and structuring
  • Real-time voice-to-EMR note generation
  • Multilingual support including Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, and more
  • Speaker diarization to separate doctor and patient speech
